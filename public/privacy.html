Privacy Policy

Folio is a local-first PDF reader. Your library is primarily stored on the device you use.

Information Folio stores

Folio uses browser storage to keep PDF files, reading progress, bookmarks, collections, notes, preferences, and downloaded smart companion model data on your device.

Folio does not operate a user account system or maintain a central library database.

Optional Google Drive sync

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Folio requests access only to its private application data folder. It may store copies of PDFs and library records there so they can sync between devices signed into the same Google account.

Google processes sign-in and Drive API requests according to Google’s own privacy terms. You can disconnect or remove Folio sync data through the app or your Google account.

Reading companion and AI

Basic reading insights are generated locally. If you download an optional smart companion model, model files and inference remain on this device and are processed in your browser. Folio does not send the text of your documents to Folio-operated servers.

Analytics and third parties

Folio does not use an analytics or advertising SDK. Loading optional Google sign-in or a downloadable model may involve a request to those providers. The app may also use normal browser service-worker and network functionality to update itself and cache resources.

Deletion and retention

You can delete individual books and collections or clear the full library from Settings. Clearing browser data may permanently remove local PDFs and reading information. Synced copies may remain in Google Drive until removed or until you disconnect and clear them.

Children’s privacy

Folio is a general-purpose reading tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect account or profile information in the app.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above shows the latest revision.

Questions or concerns: open an issue on GitHub.
